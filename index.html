MD MONIR HOSSAIN

Hello! I’m Mohammad Monir Hossain, a Textile Engineering graduate with a strong interest in materials science and advanced functional materials. My research interests include smart textiles, biopolymers and composites, nanomaterials for energy storage, triboelectric nanogenerators, hydrogel-based flexible sensors, functional textiles, MOFs, and cellulose-based thin films. I have one year of experience as a Research Assistant at Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST) under the supervision of Mr. Tarikul Islam. During this time, I gained hands-on experience in material synthesis and characterization. Currently, I am working as an R&D Officer at Youngone Corporation, where I work on developing functional and high-performance textile materials. I am now looking for PhD opportunities where I can further develop my research skills and contribute to innovative research in advanced materials, smart textiles, and functional materials.
